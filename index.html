What MetaClean removes

Your photos and videos can carry more than the visible content. MetaClean strips hidden technical data that often gets shared by accident.

Location data GPS coordinates and place information.
Camera details Device model, camera info, and technical media metadata.
Hidden identifiers Extra file metadata that most users never see.
